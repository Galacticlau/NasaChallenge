      Science Glossary

      Terms used in combustion experiments, smoke transport, and spacecraft fire safety.

      Oxygen Mole Fraction (O₂)

      The fraction of molecules in a gas mixture that are oxygen. Mole fraction is distinct from partial pressure.

      Coflow Speed

      The speed of the gas, usually air, surrounding a flame. It affects mixing, heat transport, and flame shape.

      Droplet Diameter

      A characteristic size of a fuel droplet. In droplet combustion experiments, it relates to evaporation, burning, and extinction.

      Smoke Point

      The flame length associated with the onset of visible smoke under specified test conditions. It depends on fuel, burner, and flow.

      Burning Rate

      A measure of how quickly fuel is consumed. Use the exact definition and units reported in each publication.

      Extinguishment

      The loss of self-sustaining combustion that causes a flame to go out. It can depend on gas composition, pressure, droplet size, temperature, and the suppressant used.

      Microgravity

      An environment with very low residual acceleration. Actual acceleration is not exactly zero, and the catalog also includes studies under other conditions.

      Fluctuation

      Variation over time in a signal such as temperature, flame height, or particle concentration. Quantifying fluctuation requires a time series or reported statistics such as the standard deviation.
